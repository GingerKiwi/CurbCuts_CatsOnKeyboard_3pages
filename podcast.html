Curb Cuts and Conversations

Flagship · Ongoing

A Canadian podcast about accessibility, urbanism, and transportation — how cities around the world are designed, and who gets left out when we get it wrong.

Each episode is a conversation with urban planners, researchers, disability advocates, transit experts, and people with lived experience of inaccessible cities. Host Elizabeth (Liz) McCready brings her own experience as a wheelchair user in Toronto to every conversation.

Like "curb cuts" — the small sloped transitions from sidewalk to street, designed for wheelchair users that turned out to benefit everyone — the solutions to inaccessibility are rarely just for one group. That's the curb cut effect.

Series · 6 Episodes

Adaptively Dressed: The Adaptive Fashion Podcast

Clothing designed with and for disabled, Deaf, neurodivergent, chronically ill people, and seniors. Conversations with those making, designing, promoting, and wearing adaptive fashion.

A visual and tactile series — every episode includes intentional clothing descriptions for blind and low vision listeners. Each episode opens with Liz describing herself, then she invites guests to describe themselves and what they're wearing.

The default booking is 120 minutes, with approximately 75–90 minutes of recording per episode. Pitch yourself as a guest: liz@curbcuts.fm

Series · 6 Episodes

Accessibility Across the Algorithm

Social media and digital marketing accessibility — for creators, marketers, and the audiences currently left out of the conversation. The series highlights people working to make social media accessible, and gives practical guidance on how to make your own content accessible.

Episodes are 60 minutes booked, approximately 35–45 minutes of recording. Pitch yourself as a guest: liz@curbcuts.fm

Series · Ongoing

Web Accessibility Wednesdays

10 questions in 10(ish) minutes. Short, conversational, and predictable — every guest is asked the same 10 questions. Highlighting digital accessibility professionals and their work.

30 minutes booked, approximately 10–15 minutes of recording. The question list is sent in advance — no surprises. Pitch yourself as a guest: liz@curbcuts.fm
