What is Wheelie Awesome Socials?

Wheelie Awesome Socials is a Toronto community for wheelchair users. Most events also welcome rollator and scooter users. Events include:

  • Pub nights,
  • Museum visits,
  • Crafting workshops
  • Picnics
  • Local excursions

Memberships are coming soon, with perks including member-only events, merch discounts, and an online community.
