What is Wheelie Awesome Socials?

Wheelie Awesome Socials is a Toronto community for wheelchair users — and most events welcome rollator and scooter users too. Events include:

  • pub nights,
  • museum visits,
  • crafting workshops
  • picnics
  • local excursions

Memberships are coming soon, with perks including member-only events, merch discounts, and an online community. Our first events are live — grab your tickets on Eventbrite above!
